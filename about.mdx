## **Bollywar: Bollywood Fandom Wars ⚔️**

[Bollywar](https://bollywar.com/?ref=help) is an independent entertainment community built for Bollywood fans. Whether you're rooting for your favourite superstar, debating which movie deserves the crown, or leaving a heartfelt note for a star, Bollywar gives you a place to express your opinions and connect with the world of fandom.

## Why Bollywar?

Every fan has a favourite star. Every movie has its supporters. And every great performance sparks a debate.

Bollywar brings these conversations together in one place, where fans can share their opinions, discover what others think, and enjoy friendly rivalries.

Our platform gives you the opportunity to:

- Create and participate in actor-versus-actor and movie-versus-movie battles.
- Vote in battles and see how other fans feel about your favourites.
- Like and comment on battles to join the discussion.
- Follow your favourite stars and discover battles and notes related to them.
- Explore movies and the battles created around them.
- Leave appreciation notes for stars on the Fan Wall.
- Curate your profile with the battles you've created and notes you've shared.

## Our Vision

We envision Bollywar as a place where Bollywood fandom goes beyond watching movies.

We want to bring fans together through shared interests, friendly competition, meaningful appreciation, and conversations about the stars and movies they love.

Whether you're a lifelong Bollywood enthusiast or someone who has just discovered a new favourite, there's always a place for your voice.

## An Independent Fan Community

Bollywar is an independent platform created for entertainment and fan engagement. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any actor, celebrity, production house, film studio, media organisation, public relations agency, or individual featured on the platform.

Information about stars and movies is sourced from The Movie Database ([TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org)). While we aim to provide a useful discovery experience, we do not guarantee the accuracy or timely updating of third-party information.

We believe fandom should be passionate but respectful. Behind every star, movie, and performance are real people who deserve to be treated with dignity.
