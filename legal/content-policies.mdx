## 1. Purpose

[Bollywar](https://bollywar.com/?ref=help) is a community for discussing Bollywood and other Indian entertainment, comparing actors and movies, and appreciating stars.

These Content Policies explain what users may publish on the platform. They apply to battles, comments, likes, Fan Wall notes, usernames, profile content, and other user-submitted material.

Our goal is to encourage entertaining discussions without allowing harassment, abuse, or harmful content.

## 2. Battles

Users can create battles comparing two actors or two movies by selecting the available options and submitting a question.

All battles must follow these requirements:

- The battle question must contain fewer than 150 characters.
- The selected actors or movies must match the intended comparison.
- The question must be relevant to the selected options.
- Battles must not contain threats, harassment, hate speech, or unlawful material.
- Users must not create misleading battles intended to impersonate official announcements or deceive others.
- Duplicate, spam, irrelevant, or deliberately disruptive battles may be removed.

_Battles are intended for entertainment and opinion-sharing. They should not be used to spread unverified allegations about real people._

## 3. Comments and Likes

Users may engage with battles through comments and likes.

Comments must remain relevant and respectful. Users must not use comments to:

- Attack, threaten, or intimidate another user.
- Harass actors, filmmakers, production staff, or other individuals.
- Publish personal information without authorization.
- Spread malicious rumours or present unverified allegations as established facts.
- Promote spam, scams, or unrelated commercial content.
- Encourage violence, discrimination, or unlawful activity.

Disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks are not.

_Likes and comments must not be manipulated through bots, fake accounts, or other deceptive methods._

## 4. Fan Wall

The Fan Wall allows users to share appreciation notes for their favourite stars.

- Each note must contain fewer than 250 characters.
- Notes should focus on appreciation, admiration, memorable performances, or positive messages.
- Notes must not contain harassment, threats, hateful content, explicit sexual content, or defamatory allegations.
- Users must not impersonate a celebrity or claim that a star has personally responded to or endorsed their note without a legitimate basis.
- Repeated, automated, or irrelevant submissions may be treated as spam.

_The Fan Wall is a space for fans to express appreciation, not to target or demean individuals._

## 5. Prohibited Content

The following content is not permitted on Bollywar:

**Harassment and Hate:** Content that threatens, bullies, dehumanizes, or promotes hatred or violence against individuals or groups.

**Defamation and False Claims:** Content that makes harmful, unsupported factual allegations about a real person or organization.

**Privacy Violations:** Doxxing, publishing private contact details, sharing confidential information, or exposing personal information without proper authorization.

**Sexual Exploitation:** Content involving sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate material, or any sexual content involving minors.

**Violence and Threats:** Credible threats, encouragement of violence, or content intended to intimidate others.

**Impersonation and Deception:** Fake celebrity accounts, fraudulent claims of official affiliation, deceptive endorsements, or misleading announcements.

**Spam and Manipulation:** Automated posting, repetitive content, artificial vote manipulation, scams, phishing, and malicious links.

**Intellectual Property Violations:** Content that infringes copyright, trademarks, or other intellectual property rights.

**Illegal Content:** Content that violates applicable laws or facilitates unlawful activity.

_We may also restrict content that undermines the platform's purpose or creates a significant risk to users._

## 6. Intellectual Property

Users must only submit material they have the right to use.

Do not upload, reproduce, or distribute copyrighted images, text, videos, or other material without the necessary permission or a valid legal basis.

Names, images, and information relating to stars and movies may be sourced from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org) or other third-party sources. Bollywar does not claim ownership of third-party materials merely because they appear on the platform.

## 7. Reporting Content

If you believe content violates these policies, please contact [**contact@bollywar.com**](mailto:contact@bollywar.com) with:

- A link or clear identification of the relevant content.
- A brief explanation of the suspected violation.
- Any relevant supporting information.

For privacy violations, intellectual property concerns, or formal legal complaints, contact [**legal@bollywar.com**](mailto:legal@bollywar.com).

Please provide accurate information when reporting content.

## 8. Moderation and Enforcement

Bollywar may review reported content and take appropriate action, including:

- Removing or restricting content.
- Disabling commenting or other features where necessary.
- Warning users.
- Temporarily restricting accounts.
- Suspending or permanently terminating accounts in serious or repeated cases.
- Taking further action where required by law.

We may consider the seriousness of the violation, its context, the user's history, and any risk to other people.

We reserve the right to act against harmful content even if it has not been reported.

## 9. Appeals and Review

If you believe your content was removed or your account was restricted in error, you may request a review by emailing [**contact@bollywar.com**](mailto:contact@bollywar.com).

Include the relevant content or account details and explain why you believe the decision should be reconsidered.

We will review requests where appropriate, subject to operational constraints and legal obligations.

The latest version will be available on [Bollywar.com](http://help.bollywar.com).
