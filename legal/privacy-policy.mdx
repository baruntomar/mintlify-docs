## 1. Introduction

[Bollywar](https://bollywar.com/?ref=help) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit or use [Bollywar.com](http://Bollywar.com).

Bollywar is an entertainment community where users can create and participate in battles, follow stars, publish appreciation notes, and maintain user profiles.

By using the platform, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Bollywar, we may collect the following categories of information.

### **Account Information**

- Username and registered email address.
- Account preferences and settings.

### **Content and Activity**

- Battles you create.
- Comments, likes, and Fan Wall notes you submit.
- Voting activity associated with your account.
- Stars you follow.
- Profile information and content you choose to display.

### **Technical Information**

We may collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, access timestamps, and diagnostic or security logs where necessary for website operation, security, and troubleshooting.

### **Communications**

If you contact us, we may retain your email address, message, and related correspondence to respond to your query or handle your complaint.

The information collected will depend on the features you use and the technical systems operating on the website.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Operate battles, voting, comments, likes, and Fan Wall features.
- Display user profiles and submitted content.
- Maintain private star-following functionality.
- Authenticate users and support password resets.
- Detect spam, abuse, fraud, and security incidents.
- Respond to enquiries and complaints.
- Improve platform performance and reliability.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

_We will not use personal information for purposes incompatible with this policy unless permitted or required by applicable law._

## 4. Voting and Star-Following Privacy

**Voting:** Your selected battle option is private by default. Other users cannot see your selection through the ordinary battle interface or your profile. However, if you voluntarily share a screenshot showing the "_Your pick_" indicator, others may see your selection.

**Star Following:** Your list of followed stars is private and is not displayed to other users.

These privacy features do not mean that all related information is inaccessible to Bollywar's systems. Limited records may be processed or retained as necessary for platform functionality, security, troubleshooting, or legal compliance.

## 5. Public Content

Battles, comments, likes, Fan Wall notes, usernames, and profile information may be visible to other users depending on the relevant feature and its settings.

Please do not publish sensitive personal information, confidential material, or anything you would not want other people to see.

We cannot control what other users do with information they can legitimately view, including screenshots or copies of publicly available content.

## 6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Bollywar may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to support essential website functionality, maintain sessions, remember preferences, and improve security.

If analytics or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced, their use will be subject to applicable legal requirements and any necessary consent mechanisms.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

## 7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information as a standalone product.

We may share information:

- With service providers that support hosting, authentication, security, email delivery, and website operations.
- When required by law, a court order, or a lawful request from a competent authority.
- When reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, or safeguard the platform.
- In connection with a business transfer, merger, or restructuring, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Where appropriate, service providers will be expected to handle information in accordance with applicable contractual and legal obligations.

## 8. Third-Party Services and Data

Bollywar uses information relating to stars and movies sourced from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org) and potentially other third-party sources.

These sources may have their own privacy policies and terms. Our use of third-party entertainment data does not mean that we receive private account information from the people featured in that data.

_Bollywar is an independent platform and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the celebrities, production houses, or other individuals featured on the website._

## 9. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the platform, maintain accounts, preserve relevant records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is processed.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

- Request access to personal information associated with your account.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
- Raise concerns or submit a privacy complaint.
- Exercise any additional rights available under applicable data protection law.

To submit a request, email [**legal@bollywar.com**](mailto:legal@bollywar.com). We may need to verify your identity before acting on the request.

_Some information may need to be retained where legally permitted or required, including for security, dispute resolution, and compliance purposes._

## 11. Children's Privacy

Bollywar is a general entertainment platform. Children should use the service in accordance with applicable age requirements and the laws governing their location.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where doing so is prohibited by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

## 12. International Users and Data Transfers

Bollywar may be accessed from different countries. Depending on the location of our hosting infrastructure and service providers, information may be processed or stored outside your country of residence.

Where required, we will take appropriate steps to comply with applicable rules governing international data transfers.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform develops or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on [Bollywar.com](http://Bollywar.com) with its effective date. Where legally required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes.
